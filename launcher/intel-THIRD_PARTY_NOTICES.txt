NTSD.app bundles the following open-source software, unmodified except where noted.

Wine 11.0 — https://www.winehq.org
  License: GNU LGPL 2.1 or later. Source: https://gitlab.winehq.org/wine/wine
  macOS build: https://github.com/Gcenx/macOS_Wine_builds (release 11.0_1)
  Wine Mono and Wine Gecko were removed from the bundle; an extra rpath was
  added to lib/wine/x86_64-unix/winegstreamer.so so it loads the bundled GStreamer.
  wow64cpu.dll is rebuilt from Gcenx/wine commit
  db11d0fe6a169c457e23d007e20404643d067aa8 with Rosetta-safe far-call/return
  mode transitions. The trampoline design follows tsukasa-art/swingby-wine
  commit 9c9d68d4d0b2fbf25117d9d2e737b5a9f43561ee (LGPL-2.1-or-later).
  Full corresponding Wine source, patch and build helper accompany the release.
  See licenses/ROSETTA-SOURCES.txt and licenses/Wine-COPYING.LIB.

GStreamer 1.28.6 — https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org
  License: GNU LGPL 2.1 or later (core and the bundled plugins, including mpg123).
  Source: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/gstreamer/gstreamer
  Only the libraries and plugins needed for audio playback are included.

GLib, libffi, libintl, ORC and other dependencies come from the same GStreamer
runtime package; their licenses and sources are listed at
https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/documentation/frequently-asked-questions/licensing.html

Naruto: The Setting Dawn is a free fan game by its own authors and is not covered
by the licenses above.

Window scaling uses MinHook v1.3.4, commit
c3fcafdc10146beb5919319d0683e44e3c30d537 (BSD-2-Clause, including HDE notices).
Source: https://github.com/TsudaKageyu/minhook
Unmodified source and full notices are vendored under vendor/minhook; the
application includes licenses/MinHook-LICENSE.txt.

Silent audio fallback uses DSOAL e11128250439d586483ee6598d1670ab52ab5e37
and OpenAL Soft a70d4116118cdf6bc7ac58fe6ae7a805c7205c50, LGPL-2.1-or-later.
The bundled licenses/audio-licenses directory contains their notices and fmt's.
Corresponding sources and patches accompany the GitHub release; rebuild with
 tools/build-windows-support.sh in https://github.com/wowlocal/ntsd-2.8-wine.
Sources: https://github.com/kcat/dsoal and https://github.com/kcat/openal-soft
