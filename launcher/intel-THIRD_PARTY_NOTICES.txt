NTSD.app bundles the following open-source software, unmodified except where noted.

Wine 11.0 — https://www.winehq.org
  License: GNU LGPL 2.1 or later. Source: https://gitlab.winehq.org/wine/wine
  macOS build: https://github.com/Gcenx/macOS_Wine_builds (release 11.0_1)
  Wine Mono and Wine Gecko were removed from the bundle; an extra rpath was
  added to lib/wine/x86_64-unix/winegstreamer.so so it loads the bundled GStreamer.

GStreamer 1.28.6 — https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org
  License: GNU LGPL 2.1 or later (core and the bundled plugins, including mpg123).
  Source: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/gstreamer/gstreamer
  Only the libraries and plugins needed for audio playback are included.

GLib, libffi, libintl, ORC and other dependencies come from the same GStreamer
runtime package; their licenses and sources are listed at
https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/documentation/frequently-asked-questions/licensing.html

Naruto: The Setting Dawn is a free fan game by its own authors and is not covered
by the licenses above.
